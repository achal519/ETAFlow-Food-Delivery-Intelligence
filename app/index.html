Leakage-Safe Feature Store Architecture

Strict boundary separating Tier 1 booking-time inputs from forbidden post-booking fields

✅ Tier 1: Allowed at Booking Time (Passed to Model)

These 11 attributes are strictly known when the customer confirms checkout:

distance_km (Haversine) Restaurant_latitude Restaurant_longitude Delivery_location_latitude Delivery_location_longitude order_hour [0-23] day_of_week [0-6] is_weekend [0, 1] Type_of_order (Meal, Snack...) Festival (No, Yes) City (Metropolitian, Urban...)

🚫 Forbidden Post-Booking Fields (HTTP 400 Rejected)

Attempting to pass any of these fields triggers an immediate 400 rejection to prevent data leakage:

delivery_speed (Target Leakage) Time_taken (min) (Ground Truth) Time_Order_picked actual_pickup_time actual_transit_time Delivery_person_ID Delivery_person_Ratings multiple_deliveries Type_of_vehicle
